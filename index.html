Your age picks a destination

Watch an animated route across the world — personal airport codes, shareable results, and a map of countries you have visited.

Age Destiny is a playful travel app for iOS. Choose a directory, set your departure city, enter your age, and see where you “fly” today. No account, no email — just maps and fun.

  • Age → destination Phone codes, capitals, airports & more
  • Live route map Animated arc from your city to the match
  • Your IATA code Three letters from your name initials
  • Travel diary Mark countries, years, and trip notes
  • Share Send your route to friends in one tap
  • EN · RU Light & dark themes, no signup
