Getting started

  1. Complete onboarding: date of birth, name initials (Latin letters), and departure city.
  2. On the home screen, choose a directory (for example, Capitals or Phone Codes).
  3. Adjust your age — an animated route appears from your city to the matched destination.
  4. Open My travel map to mark countries you have visited.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Age Destiny works without registration or email.

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. You can erase everything in Settings → Erase all data.

The map is empty or not loading

Map tiles require an internet connection. Check Wi‑Fi or cellular data and try again. Switching theme or map style may take a moment to reload tiles.

Are destinations travel recommendations?

No. Age Destiny is for entertainment. Matched places come from reference lists, not professional travel advice.

Which languages are supported?

English and Russian. Change language in Settings.
