Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Age Destiny (“the App”) is developed by Soluxe. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

Summary

We do not collect, sell, or store your personal data on our servers. The App works without an account. Information you enter is kept on your device unless you choose to share it (for example, via the system share sheet).

Information stored on your device

The App may store the following locally on your device:

  • Date of birth (to calculate your age)
  • Name initials (to build a personal airport-style code)
  • Selected departure city and directory preferences
  • Countries and trips you mark on your travel map, including optional notes
  • App settings such as language, theme, accent color, and map style

Travel visit data may be encrypted on device. You can delete all App data at any time in Settings → Erase all data.

Information we do not collect

  • No registration, email, or password
  • No analytics or advertising identifiers sent to us
  • No upload of your profile or travel history to Soluxe servers
  • No sale or sharing of your data with data brokers

Third-party services

The App may contact third parties only to provide functionality:

  • Map tiles — OpenStreetMap and CARTO basemaps are loaded over the internet so maps can be displayed. Requests may include standard network information (such as IP address) handled by those providers under their own policies.
  • Fonts — Google Fonts may be downloaded to render typography in the App.
  • Tourism links — If you tap a tourism website link, your browser opens that external site. We do not control third-party websites.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest version.

Contact
