2. Privacy Policy

This section explains what data the App handles and how. The App does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. We have no servers, ads, analytics, or tracking.

2.1 Data you enter (all optional)

  • Date of birth — to calculate your age;
  • Name initials — to build a personal airport-style (IATA) code;
  • Departure city and directory — for route logic;
  • Visited-countries diary — trip periods and optional notes;
  • Settings — theme, language, accent color, selected city/directory.

2.2 Where it is stored

  • Date of birth and initials are kept in the platform secure store (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), accessible only on this device after first unlock, and are not synced to iCloud.
  • The visited-countries diary is stored encrypted with AES-256; the encryption key is held in the platform secure store.
  • Settings are kept in standard local app preferences.

We have no technical means to read any of this data.

2.3 Network access

The App uses the internet only for two purposes; none of the data above is sent in these requests:

  • Map tiles from CARTO (basemaps.cartocdn.com) over HTTPS. As with any internet request, CARTO necessarily receives your device’s IP address and the map area being viewed in order to serve tiles. See carto.com/legal.
  • Directories catalogue downloaded from GitHub Pages (lazuta.github.io) over HTTPS, so new directories can appear without an app update. GitHub technically sees your IP address; only the file request is sent — none of your data.

The App contains no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party analytics.

2.4 Tracking

The App does not track you across other apps or websites and does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA / Advertising ID).

2.5 Sharing

When you tap “share”, the App hands a text message and a generated map image to the system share sheet. What happens next is controlled by you and the app you choose. The App itself uploads nothing.

2.6 Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. Because data never leaves your device, it is not shared with third parties.

2.7 Data deletion & your rights

You can erase everything at any time via Settings → Erase all data, which clears local preferences, secure storage, and the encrypted diary. Deleting the App also removes all local data. Because there is no server-side processing, you fully control your data on the device (GDPR / CCPA).

2.8 Permissions

The App requests no runtime permissions (no location, camera, contacts, microphone, or photo-library access). The only platform capability used is internet access for maps and the catalogue.

3. Cookie Policy

3.1 Does the mobile app use cookies?

No. Cookies are a web-browser technology. Age Destiny is a native mobile app and does not use cookies.

3.2 Local storage used by the app

Instead of cookies, the App uses the operating system’s local storage solely to run the App — never for advertising, tracking, or sharing data with third parties:

  • Secure store (Keychain / Keystore) — date of birth, initials, encryption key;
  • Encrypted local storage (AES-256) — visited-countries diary;
  • Local preferences — theme, language, accent, selected city/directory, catalogue cache.

All of this stays on your device and can be removed via Settings → Erase all data or by deleting the App.

3.3 This website

This website is a static informational site. It does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser may load the site’s fonts and styles, and the providers that serve them technically receive your IP address, as with any web request. If we ever introduce cookies that require consent, we will update this section and show a consent banner as required by applicable law (ePrivacy / GDPR).

3.4 Third-party services

The App loads map tiles (CARTO) and a catalogue (GitHub Pages) over HTTPS as direct requests for static files — it does not set or read those services’ cookies. Their policies: CARTO · GitHub.
